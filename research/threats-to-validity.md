# Threats to validity

- Two seeds and two search tasks provide low statistical power. Results are descriptive.
- The same provider/model generates every condition; requested seeds do not fully control model stochasticity.
- CPU timing noise remains despite serialized paired repeats and dispersion reporting.
- QueryForge adapts mechanisms to SQL; it does not reproduce paper-scale training or benchmark claims.
- The promotion threshold and workloads were frozen before principal runs.
- Eight final systems create comparison-level multiplicity despite one held-out evaluation per frozen run.
- Exact results, types and safety allowlists reduce evaluator gaming but cannot establish universal SQL equivalence.
- Subscription execution exposes no per-call dollar invoice, so dollar-cost comparison is unavailable.
